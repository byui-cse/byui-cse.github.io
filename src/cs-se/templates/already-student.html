Already a Student?

Switching, Transferring, or Coming Back? Start Here.

Plenty of great engineers found computing a little later. Here is how to jump in without losing momentum.

  1. Try the First Course

    Take CSE 110 Introduction to Programming. It is the first course in both majors and the fastest way to find out if you love this. Already programming? Test out with the LUC placement test and start in CSE 111.

  2. Talk with an Advisor

    An advisor can show how your current credits fit and map out your semesters. Meet with Academic Advising.

  3. Transferring Credit?

    See how courses from another school may count with the course transfer tool. Official evaluation decides what applies.

  4. Coming Back after Time Away?

    Whether you served a mission, worked, or cared for family, there is a path back. Former student admission.

Current students can also use the Student Hub for tools built for BYU-Idaho students. Questions about computing? Contact the CSE Advising Office at STEMadvising@byui.edu or 208-496-9880.
