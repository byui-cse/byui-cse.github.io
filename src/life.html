The Computing Society

Wednesday Nights Are for Building

Every Wednesday at 5:00 PM, the STC quad (rooms 375, 385, 394, and 398) becomes a hangout space for builders. Grab some food, open your laptop, and hack on whatever you are into: Your own project, a class assignment, or something brand new with friends who love cyber defense, cloud apps, data, drones, even a hybrid electric race car.

No experience needed and no tryouts. Show up your first semester and walk away with friends, projects for your portfolio, and probably a new favorite hobby.
